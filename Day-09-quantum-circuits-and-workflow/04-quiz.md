## Question 3

Why does depth matter on noisy hardware?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Longer sequential execution increases exposure to error and decoherence.

</details>

## Question 4

What is transpilation?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Rewriting a logical circuit for a specific backend's gate set and constraints.

</details>
