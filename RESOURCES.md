## Research reading habit

For any paper or benchmark:

1. Identify the problem and baseline.
2. Locate the exact quantum resource model.
3. Separate simulator results from hardware results.
4. Check qubit count, depth, shots, and noise assumptions.
5. Verify how classical preprocessing and post-processing are counted.
6. Look for independent reproduction or strong follow-up evidence.
7. Write down what evidence would falsify the claim.
