## Question 1

What is the quantum subproblem at the heart of Shor's algorithm?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Period/order finding.

</details>

## Question 2

What transform is central to extracting the period?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

QFT or an equivalent phase-estimation structure.

</details>

## Question 3

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No.

</details>

## Question 4

What kind of quantum computer is required for cryptographically large instances?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A large fault-tolerant quantum computer.

</details>

## Question 5

What should organizations do now?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Plan and execute cryptographic inventory and post-quantum migration rather than waiting for a future machine.

</details>
