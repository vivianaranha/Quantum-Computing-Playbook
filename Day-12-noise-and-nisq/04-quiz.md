## Question 1

What does NISQ emphasize?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Useful but noisy quantum processors without large-scale fault tolerance.

</details>

## Question 2

What type of error changes |0⟩ and |1⟩ populations?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Bit-flip-like error.

</details>

## Question 3

What type of error damages phase relationships?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Dephasing or phase-flip-like error.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No.

</details>
