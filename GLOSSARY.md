# Quantum Computing Glossary

## Amplitude
A complex coefficient associated with a basis state. Its squared magnitude contributes to measurement probability.

## Ansatz
A parameterized trial circuit used in a variational algorithm.

## Ancilla
An auxiliary qubit used for computation, measurement, error detection, or control.

## Bell state
One of four maximally entangled two-qubit states.

## Bloch sphere
A geometric representation of pure single-qubit states.

## Circuit depth
The number of sequential operation layers in a circuit under a chosen scheduling model.

## CNOT
A two-qubit controlled-X gate.

## Coherence
The persistence of quantum phase relationships needed for interference.

## Decoherence
Loss of quantum information through interaction with the environment.

## Entanglement
Non-separable quantum correlation between subsystems.

## Expectation value
The average value expected when measuring an observable over repeated preparations.

## Fidelity
A measure of similarity between intended and actual quantum states or operations.

## Hamiltonian
An operator representing system energy and governing time evolution.

## Interference
The addition or cancellation of quantum amplitudes based on relative phase.

## Logical qubit
An error-protected qubit encoded across multiple physical qubits.

## Measurement
An operation that converts quantum information into classical outcomes according to the measurement basis.

## NISQ
Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum: imperfect quantum processors without large-scale fault tolerance.

## Oracle
A reversible operation that encodes a function queried by a quantum algorithm.

## QEC
Quantum error correction.

## QFT
Quantum Fourier Transform.

## QPE
Quantum Phase Estimation.

## QUBO
Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization.

## Qubit
The basic unit of quantum information.

## Shot
One sampled execution of a quantum circuit measurement.

## Superposition
A coherent combination of basis states described by amplitudes.

## Transpilation
Compilation of a logical circuit to a target backend's gates and connectivity.

## Unitary
A norm-preserving linear operator representing ideal closed-system quantum evolution.

## VQE
Variational Quantum Eigensolver.
