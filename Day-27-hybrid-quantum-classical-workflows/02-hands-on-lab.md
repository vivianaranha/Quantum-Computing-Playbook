# Day 27 Hands-On Lab — Hybrid Quantum-Classical Workflows

## Objective

Design a reference architecture for a hybrid optimization service with API, preprocessing, classical baseline, circuit service, quantum backend, result store, evaluation, and fallback.

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Predict
Before doing any calculation or simulation, write down what you expect to observe and why.

### Step 2 — Build or derive
Work through the circuit, state, model, comparison, or architecture for today's topic. Show intermediate steps rather than jumping directly to the answer.

### Step 3 — Test
Use hand calculations, a simulator, a small Python script, or your chosen quantum SDK where appropriate.

### Step 4 — Compare
Compare the observed result with your prediction.

### Step 5 — Explain deviations
If the result differs, classify the cause:

- Conceptual error
- Algebra mistake
- Qubit / bit ordering
- Measurement basis
- Finite-shot sampling
- Transpilation or gate convention
- Hardware noise
- Tooling / implementation issue

### Step 6 — Record
Add the following to your lab notes:

- Goal
- Prediction
- Method
- Result
- What surprised me
- What I would change
- One follow-up experiment

## Stretch challenge

Change one assumption or parameter and predict the new outcome before testing it.

## Definition of done

You are done when another learner could reproduce your reasoning from the notes alone.
