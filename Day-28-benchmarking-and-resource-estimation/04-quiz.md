# Day 28 Quiz — Benchmarking, Complexity, and Resource Estimation

Answer first. Then reveal the answer.

## Question 1

What is a resource estimate?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A quantified projection of computational and hardware requirements for an algorithm.

</details>

## Question 2

Why is T-count important?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Non-Clifford T operations can dominate fault-tolerant overhead.

</details>

## Question 3

What should a benchmark make reproducible?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Data, circuit, compiler, backend, execution settings, and analysis.

</details>

## Question 4

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Yes.

</details>

## Question 5

What is the most dangerous benchmark mistake?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Comparing against an inappropriate or weak classical baseline.

</details>
