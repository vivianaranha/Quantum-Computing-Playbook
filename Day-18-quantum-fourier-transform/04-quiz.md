# Day 18 Quiz — Quantum Fourier Transform

Answer first. Then reveal the answer.

## Question 1

Which gates dominate the textbook QFT structure?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Hadamards and controlled phase rotations.

</details>

## Question 2

What does inverse QFT do?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Reverses the QFT transformation.

</details>

## Question 3

Is QFT equivalent to taking a classical FFT of read-out data?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No; it transforms quantum amplitudes coherently before measurement.

</details>

## Question 4

Why are bit-reversal swaps sometimes present?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

To match output qubit ordering conventions.

</details>

## Question 5

What major algorithmic primitive depends on QFT?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Quantum phase estimation.

</details>
