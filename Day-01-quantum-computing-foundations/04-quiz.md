# Day 01 Quiz — Quantum Computing Foundations

Answer first. Then reveal the answer.

## Question 1

What is the basic unit of quantum information?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A qubit.

</details>

## Question 2

Does superposition mean a qubit literally stores two classical answers that can both be read out?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No. Measurement produces a classical outcome according to the state's probabilities.

</details>

## Question 3

Name three ideas central to quantum computation.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Superposition, interference, and entanglement.

</details>

## Question 4

What must a useful quantum-advantage claim compare against?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A relevant classical baseline.

</details>

## Question 5

Why are hybrid workflows important today?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Classical systems remain strong for orchestration, optimization loops, preprocessing, and post-processing while quantum processors handle selected subroutines.

</details>
