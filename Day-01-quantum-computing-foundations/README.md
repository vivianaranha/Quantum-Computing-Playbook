# Day 01 — Quantum Computing Foundations

## Goal

Build a mental model of what quantum computing is, what it is not, and where it can matter.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of today, you should be able to:

- Explain **quantum computing in one sentence** in your own words.
- Explain **why quantum is different** in your own words.
- Explain **quantum advantage** in your own words.
- Explain **what quantum does not mean** in your own words.
- Complete the day's hands-on activity.
- Produce the day's build artifact.
