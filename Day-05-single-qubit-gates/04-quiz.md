Answer first. Then reveal the answer.

## Question 1

Which gate is the closest quantum analogue of NOT?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

X.

</details>

## Question 2

What state is H|0⟩?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

|+⟩ = (|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2.

</details>

## Question 3

What is H(H|ψ⟩)?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

|ψ⟩, because H²=I.

</details>

## Question 4

Does Z change the Z-basis measurement probabilities of an arbitrary state immediately?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No; it changes relative phase.

</details>

## Question 5

Why is T strategically important?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

It is a non-Clifford gate and often dominates fault-tolerant resource costs.

</details>
