Answer first. Then reveal the answer.

## Question 1

Name two gate-model hardware modalities.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Examples: superconducting qubits, trapped ions, neutral atoms, photonics, spin qubits.

</details>

## Question 2

Why is fidelity important?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Errors compound across operations and limit useful circuit depth.

</details>

## Question 3

Does the highest qubit count automatically mean the best processor?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No.

</details>

## Question 4

What does connectivity describe?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Which qubits can directly interact through native operations.

</details>

## Question 5

Why should application teams care about modality?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

It affects available gates, connectivity, fidelity, latency, scale, and deployment assumptions.

</details>
