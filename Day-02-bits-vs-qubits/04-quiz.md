# Day 02 Quiz — Bits vs Qubits

Answer first. Then reveal the answer.

## Question 1

What equation must α and β satisfy?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

|α|² + |β|² = 1.

</details>

## Question 2

What does |α|² represent?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

The probability of measuring 0 in the computational basis.

</details>

## Question 3

Is relative phase observable directly in one computational-basis measurement?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No, but it can affect later interference.

</details>

## Question 4

Where is |0⟩ on the Bloch sphere?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

At the north pole in the common convention.

</details>

## Question 5

Can the Bloch sphere directly visualize a general two-qubit state?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No.

</details>
