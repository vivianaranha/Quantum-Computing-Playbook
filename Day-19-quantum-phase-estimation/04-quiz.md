# Day 19 Quiz — Quantum Phase Estimation

Answer first. Then reveal the answer.

## Question 1

What does QPE estimate?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

The phase associated with an eigenvalue of a unitary.

</details>

## Question 2

What must be known or prepared for the target register?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

An eigenstate, or a state with useful overlap on eigenstates.

</details>

## Question 3

What converts phase encoding into classical bits?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Inverse QFT followed by measurement.

</details>

## Question 4

How does more counting-register precision affect resources?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

It generally requires more qubits and deeper controlled evolution.

</details>

## Question 5

Why is QPE foundational?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Many advanced quantum algorithms reduce important quantities to eigenphase estimation.

</details>
