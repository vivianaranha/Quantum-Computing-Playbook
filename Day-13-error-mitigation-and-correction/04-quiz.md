## Question 1

Does error mitigation create a fully fault-tolerant computer?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No.

</details>

## Question 2

What is encoded across many physical qubits in QEC?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A logical qubit.

</details>

## Question 3

What does a syndrome reveal?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Information about likely errors, not the logical value itself.

</details>

## Question 4

Why is overhead a central QEC concern?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Protection requires many additional physical resources.

</details>

## Question 5

What theorem prevents arbitrary cloning of unknown quantum states?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

The no-cloning theorem.

</details>
