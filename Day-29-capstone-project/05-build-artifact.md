## Today's deliverable

Complete the capstone package: `README.md`, `problem.md`, `method.md`, `results.md`, `limitations.md`, and `next-steps.md`.

## Why build an artifact every day?

A daily artifact turns passive reading into visible evidence of learning. By Day 30, you will have a set of small references, explainers, decision guides, and project components that can be reused in your capstone.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Written in my own words
- [ ] Includes at least one concrete example
- [ ] Separates ideal theory from hardware reality where relevant
- [ ] States assumptions
- [ ] Avoids exaggerated claims
- [ ] Could be understood by someone who completed the prior days
- [ ] Includes one question or next experiment
