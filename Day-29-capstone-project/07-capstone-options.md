# Capstone Options

Choose one.

## Option A — Grover Search
Build a small marked-item search, explain amplitude amplification, compare query counts, and discuss practical limits.

## Option B — QAOA MaxCut
Map a graph to a binary objective, build a shallow QAOA structure, compare against brute force or a classical heuristic, and discuss parameter sensitivity.

## Option C — VQE Toy Hamiltonian
Choose a tiny Hamiltonian, design an ansatz, estimate expectations, optimize parameters, and compare against exact diagonalization.

## Option D — Quantum Teleportation
Implement and explain teleportation, verify output states across multiple inputs, and discuss why classical communication is necessary.

## Option E — Noise Study
Run the same small circuit under ideal and noisy conditions, vary depth or noise level, and analyze degradation.

## Required deliverables

- Problem statement
- Background
- Classical baseline
- Quantum method
- Experiment design
- Results
- Limitations
- Reproducibility notes
- Next steps
