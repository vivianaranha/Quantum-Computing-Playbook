# Day 07 Quiz — Multi-Qubit States and Tensor Products

Answer first. Then reveal the answer.

## Question 1

How many amplitudes describe a general pure state of n qubits?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

2^n complex amplitudes.

</details>

## Question 2

Is every correlated state entangled?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No.

</details>

## Question 3

What operation combines state spaces?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

The tensor product.

</details>

## Question 4

Can a product state still produce nontrivial measurement statistics?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Yes.

</details>

## Question 5

Why do qubit-ordering conventions matter?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

They change how vector indices and printed bitstrings map to logical qubits.

</details>
